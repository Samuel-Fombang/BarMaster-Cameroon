# BarMaster Cameroon

# Development Roadmap

Version: 1.0

Author: Samuel Fombang

# Project Vision

BarMaster Cameroon is a professional inventory, sales, and business management system designed for bars, lounges, snack bars, and restaurants.

The objective is to manage the complete business workflow from purchasing drinks to generating business reports.

---

# Development Methodology
